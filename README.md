# ultrastable/spec

**Architectural specifications and documentation for Ultrastable**: a system that turns a person who can only say what they want into a managed, self-correcting build, by writing and following its own specifications, charters and plans.

> **License: free for non-commercial use; commercial use (including commercial implementations of these specifications) requires a license and shares revenue with the Licensor.** [LICENSE](LICENSE), [COMMERCIAL-TERMS.md](COMMERCIAL-TERMS.md). Source-available, not open source. The specifications are dated 2026-11-11 and published openly as a public record of the design.
